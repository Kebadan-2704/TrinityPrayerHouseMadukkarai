Who We Are

About Trinity

A Spirit-filled church family rooted in the love of Jesus Christ, serving Madukkarai since our founding.

Our Story

Welcome to
Trinity Prayer House

Trinity Prayer House Madukkarai is a vibrant, Spirit-filled Christian community located in the heart of Madukkarai, Coimbatore. We are a church that believes the Bible is the living Word of God, that Jesus Christ is Lord and Saviour, and that the Holy Spirit is actively moving in the world today.

Our church was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to be a place of genuine encounter with God — where people from all walks of life can come as they are and leave transformed by His grace.

From our Sunday worship services to our midweek gatherings, community outreach, and youth programmes, everything we do flows from our love for God and our love for the people of Madukkarai.

For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them. — Matthew 18:20
Purpose

Our Vision & Values

📖

Rooted in the Word

We believe the Bible is the living, authoritative Word of God. Our teaching is grounded in Scripture, practical for everyday life, and delivered with clarity and love.

🔥

Spirit-Led Worship

We cultivate an atmosphere where the Holy Spirit moves freely. Authentic praise, heartfelt prayer, and the presence of God are the heartbeat of our gatherings.

🤝

Community & Fellowship

We love and serve one another. From the youngest child to the oldest member, everyone belongs here and every story matters in the family of God.

🌍

Outreach & Mission

Called to go beyond our walls — we serve the people of Madukkarai and beyond with the compassion, generosity, and love of Christ in practical ways.

🙏

A House of Prayer

Prayer is not just something we do — it is who we are. We believe in the power of prayer to transform hearts, heal lives, and change communities.

✝

Christ at the Centre

Everything we do centres on the person and work of Jesus Christ — his life, death, resurrection, and coming return. He is our hope, our peace, and our salvation.

✝
Leadership

Pastor's Welcome

Senior Pastor · Trinity Prayer House, Madukkarai

We are so glad you found us. Trinity Prayer House exists to be a lighthouse in Madukkarai — a place where the broken are healed, the lost are found, and the faithful are strengthened in their walk with God.

No matter where you are in your journey — whether you've walked with Christ for decades or are just beginning to ask questions about faith — our doors and hearts are open to you. Come, experience God's love, and grow with us as a family in Christ.

We believe that church is not simply a building or a Sunday service. It is a living community of people committed to loving God, loving one another, and loving our neighbours. That is Trinity Prayer House.

In His Service,
The Pastoral Team, Trinity Prayer House
